Structs Command Deck

Play Structs, the space strategy game on the Cosmos blockchain, in your browser: a Dreamcast-style client that works on phones, keyboards and gamepads, with an Academy that teaches strategy, economics and technology as you play.

Structs is the game where sentient machines fight over Alpha Matter. This client runs the whole game in a browser tab: watch the live galaxy and every real battle without an account, practise in the Battle Simulator, learn in the Academy, meet other commanders in the Concourse, and, once you enlist, play for real with your keys sealed in the tab.

Base: your planet, your robots, your next move

  • My Base: build, mine and give orders. The advisor says what to do next.
  • Rank & Missions: your rank, your XP and the mission ladder.
  • Moves in Progress: every move you have sent, and whether it landed.

Battle: practise tactics, then watch real fights

  • Training Battles: your fleet against the computer. No account needed.
  • Daily Challenge: one new battle a day, the same for every player.
  • Battle Replays: every real attack in the galaxy, replayed shot by shot.
  • Unit Guide: all 22 units: what each one hits, and what hits it.

Explore, Trade, Team and Lab

  • Galaxy Map and Power Grid: every world and the energy economy, live from the chain.
  • Economy: power, fuel, the market, banks and transfers.
  • Guild Hall, Intel and Comms: your team, the guild data layer and chat.
  • The Forge and Operations: every chain message as a form; the queue, proofs and robot rules.

Learn

  • Strategy, Economics and Technology: short lessons with quizzes and things to try.
  • Glossary: every game word in plain language.
