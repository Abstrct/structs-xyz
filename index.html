Structs Command Deck

A full browser client for Structs, the 5X space strategy game on the Cosmos blockchain where sentient machines fight over Alpha Matter. Build, mine, refine and raid on chain with proofs ground on your GPU; run replicants; chat with your guild; read the guild data layer; watch the live galaxy and replay every real attack.

Watch, no keys needed

  • Live Galaxy: every planet ever discovered on the testnet, guild-lit homes, raids in flight.
  • Combat Theatre: every attack ever fought, replayed shot by shot from the chain.
  • The Grid: reactors, substations and every allocation in the energy economy.
  • Battle Simulator: fleet-versus-computer battles on the real combat rules.
  • Codex: all 22 struct types with full mechanics and matchups.
  • Intel: the guild data layer, live.

Play

  • Command Deck: your commander, every struct, every action.
  • Operations: the transaction queue, proof-of-work engine and replicants.
  • The Forge: every chain message as a form.
  • Economy: power, fuel, the market, banks and transfers.
  • Comms: guild chat over Matrix.
  • Guild Hall: rosters, ranks, membership and permissions.
